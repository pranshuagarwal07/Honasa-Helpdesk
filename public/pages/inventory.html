Shelf bins

Empties whole bins. Pallet bins: a pallet's cases go onto a pallet of the same stock that has room, or the pallet moves whole into a free position. Shelf bins: stock is poured by volume into bins that already hold stock. Bins with arriving or locked stock, stock that isn't INCLUDED, and fixed bins are left alone.

Frees pallet positions inside bins: merges cases of the same SKU, batch, bucket, owner and inclusion status onto the fullest pallet, up to its best fit (the SKU's L2inL3), even where the bin keeps other pallets. Pallet bins only.

Every move is checked against the WMS capacity check the IM upload runs, pallet positions, bin and pallet mixing rules (multi-SKU, multi-batch, max SKU), max kg if ticked, the search table for another zone, task locks and fixed bins, then replayed by an independent re-check. Reads the stock now; nothing is moved.
